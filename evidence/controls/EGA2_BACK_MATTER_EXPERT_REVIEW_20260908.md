# EGA II Korean back-matter: expert terminology and provenance review

## Scope and evidentiary status

This record covers one complete back-matter tranche: the bibliography continuation, notation index, all 105 terminological-index entries, the original contents, and *Errata et Addenda (Liste 1)*. It documents translation choices; it is not a QA pass, certification, or claim of external review. The companion JSONL contains 105 individually located terminological-index occurrences and 38 substantive bibliography, notation, contents, and addenda records, plus one metadata record.

The French files remain diplomatic authority for printed content, formulae, numbering, page markers, and structure. The maintained English files are semantic witnesses only. Their useful clarifications were considered, but witness-only proof wrappers, `Hom_rs`, altered page-marker placement, and lossy type substitutions were not treated as source facts. Bibliographic titles remain in their published languages. The standalone-reader copies of [1], [9], [11], and [13] are explicitly marked as editorial supplements; the printed EGA II continuation itself is [23]–[26].

## Korean canon actually used

The governing Korean evidence is the existing EGA register in `terms.jsonl` and the exact live wording at each referenced `ega/II/c2s1.tex` label. The index was therefore treated as a second occurrence of an established choice, not as a place to invent synonyms. The occurrence map binds the exact French headword, exact Korean candidate headword, candidate line, live label and line range, governing term/decision IDs, and the precise canon passages consulted.

The read arXiv loci—`1803.01127v1` lines 23–30, 103, 217–222, 258–267; `2301.11539v2` lines 527, 632–684; and `1603.00618v2` lines 164–170, 213–220, 271–285—serve only as English semantic/type controls. They support distinctions involving coherent and ideal sheaves, locally free resolutions, projective bundles, cokernels, flatness, blow-ups, contraction, restriction, and positivity. They do not authorize Korean spelling or mathematical glyphs.

The earlier bounded search found no qualifying Korean-language algebraic-geometry paper. That is evidence of scarcity in the searched set, not proof of nonexistence. Consequently, `준스킴`, `국소환 달린 공간`, `국소 준동형`, `아핀 스킴`, `쌍함자`, `디-준동형`, `준연접`, `연접`, `국소 자유`, `잉여체`, `우세 사상`, and `분수체` are reported as source-aligned project-register choices backed by the cited Korean professional sources already bound in the ledger—not as community-certified universal forms.

## Reviewable debt and nonaccepted leads

Several points remain deliberately visible rather than silently resolved:

- The terminology index and candidate print `G(\mathcal E)`, while the referenced French main text and live Korean locus use `G(\varepsilon)`. This is a formula-identity, scan-first lead; the occurrence has low editorial confidence pending adjudication.
- Korean determinant terminology is split between `결정식` in the term-index candidate and `행렬식` in the contents/live heading. The mathematics is fixed; the lexical normalization is not.
- `기본대칭함수` and `기본 대칭함수` both occur in the live register. The candidate uses the closed form, but the spacing is not claimed settled.
- The graded-module headword `Type fini` is rendered `유한형` in the candidate, while `AGKO-T006` normally distinguishes module/algebraic finite generation as `유한 생성` from geometric `유한형`. This occurrence is explicitly low-confidence pending aggregate disposition.
- The theorem name appears as `저우` in the terminological index and `차우` in the contents. No external Korean algebraic-geometry attestation was found to settle the transliteration.
- The homogeneous-spectrum index uses generic base `\mathcal O_S`; its referenced construction uses `\mathcal O_Y`. This is recorded as base-letter/editorial variation, not silently rewritten.
- The contents follows French/current Korean `\mathcal O_X` in §6.4; the English witness says `\mathcal A`. No accepted disposition supports replacing the French/live type.
- Adjacent contents headings spell Grauert as both `그라우어트` and `그라우에르트`; normalization remains open.
- `Errata et Addenda` is `정오표 및 추록` in the translated heading but `정오표 및 추가 사항` in the contents. The provenance is clear; wording consistency remains open.
- In the discussion following 1.8.10, French calls 1.8.7 “la proposition” although 1.8.7 is a corollary. The Korean candidate retains `명제` pending an accepted scan-first disposition.

The English witness's `\mathcal A` substitution for the normed invertible `\mathcal B`-modules is rejected because French and II.6.5 typing require `\mathcal B`. Its replacement of `\mathcal O_{\mathfrak X}` by `\mathcal O_X` at the terminal addendum is likewise not followed. The source-controlled fraktur object and `연접 아이디얼층` are preserved.
