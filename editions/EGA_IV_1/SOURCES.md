# Sources and authorship

Original: Alexandre Grothendieck, with Jean Dieudonné, *Éléments de géométrie algébrique IV*, first part, Publications Mathématiques de l’IHÉS 20 (1964), pp. 5–259. [French original](https://numdam.org/item/PMIHES_1964__20__5_0/).

The translation is AI-translated; the subsequent corrections and typesetting are AI-produced. The publisher did not translate the text. Recorded work includes OpenAI Codex, GPT-5.6 Sol and GPT-6 Astra, Ultra. Original authorship and cited human contributions are retained.

The French TeX mathematical notation and source labels are retained as part of the source framework. The [French EGA edition](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/ega-fr/releases/tag/ega-fr-2026-10-24-r19) and the [human community English translation](https://github.com/ryankeleti/ega/tree/3d7afe28c99d1887ccdb85acf2ca1afd93fb1971) provide separately credited reference material; the latter names Mateo Carmona, Tim Holzschuh, Tim Hosgood, Ryan Keleti, Thiago Solovera & Nery and Wayne Yang. These contributors are not credited as translators of the Chinese or Korean text. See the upstream [section credits](https://github.com/ryankeleti/ega/blob/3d7afe28c99d1887ccdb85acf2ca1afd93fb1971/README.md) for the scope of their English work.
