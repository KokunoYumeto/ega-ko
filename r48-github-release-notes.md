# Korean cumulative EGA — 2026-09-07-r48

This cumulative release contains every Korean EGA passage admitted so far: complete EGA 0_I and EGA I, the complete EGA II Chapter II programme and table of contents, and uninterrupted EGA II main text through §2.5.12 (canonical French `ega2-1-fr.tex` lines 1–2694).

R40–R48 were integrated as one contiguous source-ordered batch. The new mathematics runs from §2.2.7 through §2.5.12 and covers the homogeneous prime spectrum, standard opens and the topology of `Proj(S)`, its scheme structure, functorial constructions from graded rings, and sheaves associated with graded modules.

The front reader is 249 A4 pages, 1,542,495 bytes, SHA-256 `668E8008D24C5B676D18BCA933D3DD57159877F2B45BD778EA1CE28204A4623E`. One mutex-held convergence build and one aggregate whole-reader QA were performed; there were no per-section or repeated QA loops. The checks found no unresolved source hyperlink, fatal TeX diagnostic, replacement character, unembedded font, non-A4 page, clipping, overlap, tofu, black box, damaged formula/diagram, or unreadable text in the inspected newly affected pages.

This is a worthwhile cumulative checkpoint, not a completion claim. EGA II continues at Proposition 2.5.13 after blank canonical line 2695. Editable TeX and rendered formulae remain authoritative where legacy mathematics-font extraction is lossy.
